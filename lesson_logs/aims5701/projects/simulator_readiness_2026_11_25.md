# AIMS5701 · Simulator readiness, 25 November 2026

**Project evidence.** The feasibility pilot reached GO for the synthetic 25% to 60% Java routing intervention. The telemetry study reached READY for testing competing estimators under a five-second, outbox-only observation contract. An independent audit reconstructed 3,200 protected continuations. Among 1,200 ordinary protected pairs, promoting breached in 835 and holding avoided 370 of them on the matched futures. [Original pilot report](https://github.com/alistairkung/particle-filter-deployment-gate/blob/bb20ebed72471f43443c873a11b2ac6e32fbbc00/feasibility_pilot/FEASIBILITY_REPORT.md) · [Original readiness report](https://github.com/alistairkung/particle-filter-deployment-gate/blob/624541bae2405fe47a59a193a55482775a7e0d0a/telemetry_sufficiency/results/v1/Experimental_Readiness_Report.md)

**What the outbox revealed.** Held-out histories improved realised-demand MSE by 0.000834 and incremental-risk MSE by 0.003591 against the best constant. Both simultaneous lower bounds exceeded zero. Distinguishing operating conditions drove the gains. Within conditions the corresponding constant means performed better. Publisher slowdown was unusually easy to identify, and the evaluator with privileged physical inputs forecast risk better.

**Open question.** History improved absolute breach forecasts for each action. Its incremental-risk MSE gain over the latest complete observation was 0.00146 (descriptive 95% interval −0.00076 to +0.00362). Identical prefixes could not warn of a surprise increase starting at the decision. Particle filtering must establish any additional value against these baselines on fresh independent protected root families.
